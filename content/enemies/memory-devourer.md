## Behavior / AI
Chapter 1 fight = **SEQ 11 expansion, three phases woven into the battle waves:**
1. **Watching** — visible only to Kai, standing in the desaturated seams of the battlefield while the real battle rages saturated. Player must *choose* to step off history's stage into a seam. Limot spawn behind as the price of looking away.
2. **The seams** — hit-and-run across 3 rift pockets; each damage cycle = defending a memory anchor (a crowd-face, a song-phrase, the ancestor's face) while Diwa-bursting the rift. Pure survival teaching: the player's whole Phase-1 kit in one exam.
3. **The pull-back** — as the battle's recorded ending begins, the Devourer reaches for the *event itself*; Kai's burst seals the rift — and the goodbye with CH_0002 happens in this phase's pause (storyline SEQ 12's "You came." lands here). **It is expelled, never killed** (Beat Rule 4).

**The no-fight rule:** if the player never enters a seam in phase 1, the battle still plays out — but stability keeps draining and phase 2 auto-enters with less Diwa. It can be delayed, not skipped.
