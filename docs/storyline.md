# BAYANI — CHAPTER 01: MACTAN

## **“The Blood Remembers”**

### Story Premise

Kai is a young person living in the future who becomes obsessed with their family's forgotten history.

For years, Kai has felt that something is wrong with their family.

Their parents avoid talking about their ancestors. Old family records are incomplete. An ancient artifact has been passed down through generations, but nobody will explain where it came from.

Kai only knows one thing:

> **“Our family has been here before.”**

After an argument with their parents, Kai runs away.

Lost near an old coastal ruin, Kai discovers the artifact.

When Kai touches it, the world breaks apart.

And Kai is thrown into the year **1521**.

But Kai doesn't arrive randomly.

They arrive during one of the most important moments in Philippine history.

---

# SCENE 01 — THE QUESTION

### Location

Future Philippines — Kai's home.

### Story

Kai is looking through old family photographs.

There are pictures of their parents, grandparents, and relatives.

But one section of the family tree is completely blank.

Kai asks their parent:

> **Kai:** “Why don't we have anything before Lolo?”

Their parent immediately closes the old family book.

> **Parent:** “Some things are better left in the past.”

Kai:

> **“Why?”**

No answer.

Kai notices an ancient symbol carved into the family artifact.

It looks strangely familiar.

### Visual

* Modern Filipino home
* Family photographs
* Digital devices
* Old wooden chest
* Family genealogy displayed on a screen
* Artifact inside an old cloth

### Effects

Subtle glitch whenever Kai looks at the artifact.

### Narration

> “I knew the names of the people who came before me.”
>
> “But I didn't know their story.”
>
> “And the more I asked...”
>
> “the more my family wanted me to forget.”

# SCENE 02 — THE ARGUMENT

Kai confronts their parents.

> **Kai:** “You're hiding something from me.”

Parent:

> “Kai, stop asking questions.”

Kai:

> “It's my family.”

Parent:

> “You don't understand.”

Kai:

> “Then explain it to me!”

The parent grabs the artifact.

For the first time, Kai sees fear in their parent's eyes.

> **Parent:** “Never use this.”

Kai:

> “Why?”

Parent:

> **“Because it doesn't bring people back.”**

Kai:

> “Then what does it do?”

Silence.

Kai leaves the house.

# SCENE 03 — RUNNING AWAY

Kai runs through the city.

They reach a remote coastal area.

**NO SERVICE.**

Future city:

**Buildings → roads → vegetation → ruins → cave**

### Narration

>
> “I didn't know I was running toward them.”

# The Important Twist

I would make the twist **not** simply "Kai changed history."

Instead:

### **Kai's journey is creating a broken timeline.**

Every time Kai travels into the past, they recover a forgotten piece of Filipino history—but when the artifact returns Kai to the future, **the present changes slightly.**

So the overarching game mystery becomes:

> **Why is Kai's future changing?**

And eventually Kai discovers:

**The artifact isn't taking Kai through history.**

**It is taking Kai through the memories of their bloodline.**

Each chapter reveals another ancestor, another historical period, and another piece of the reason Kai's family has been erased from the future.
