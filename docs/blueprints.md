
> ## 📎 PRODUCTION & NARRATIVE APPENDIX
> This document is **supporting material**. The canonical spec is [`ggd.md`](ggd.md) — see its **§56 Canonical Resolutions**. Where they differ, ggd.md wins. Notably superseded here: **§50 playtime** (slice is 60–90 min; public demo 20–30 min), **§41–43 Laravel/MySQL tables** (post-slice mirrors for telemetry only — ScriptableObjects are the single content source of truth), **§34 boss name** (canonical: The Memory Devourer — see note below), treasure rarity uses the canonical 5 tiers (Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Mythic).

One historical correction that matters for the opening: **Limasawa → Cebu → Mactan** is a better sequence than treating “first land of the Spaniards” as a single location. NHCP records the expedition reaching Mazaua/Limasawa on March 28, 1521, the Easter Sunday mass on March 31, departure April 4, and later the Mactan conflict on April 27. ([Philippine Historical Sites][1])

Also, the artifact system can be grounded in actual Philippine archaeological categories—earthenware, foreign ceramics, ornaments, tools, burial goods, etc.—while the supernatural items remain clearly marked as **mythological/game-fiction objects**. The National Museum documents extensive archaeological collections in exactly these categories. ([National Museum of the Philippines][2])

# 29. HISTORICAL CONTEXT

The NHCP describes Lapulapu as one of the leaders of Mactan and records that he resisted the authority asserted by Magellan and led the defense of Mactan on April 27, 1521. ([Philippine Historical Sites][6])

For the game, we can build the surrounding adventure around this documented event without claiming that Kai or supernatural creatures participated in the historical battle.

# 52. BAYANI'S UNIQUE IDENTITY

The strongest combination is:

**Future Filipino kid**

*

**Philippine history**

*

**Action RPG**

*

**Filipino mythology**

*

**Artifacts & treasure hunting**

*

**Time travel**

*

**Narrative mystery**

That is much more distinctive than simply making a game about Lapu-Lapu.

And the artifact/cultural layer has a real foundation: the National Museum's archaeological collections document material evidence ranging from ceramics and tools to ornaments, burial goods and foreign trade wares. ([National Museum of the Philippines][7])

**The first milestone should therefore be `BAYANI: Echoes of Mactan — Vertical Slice`, not the entire game.** Once that one-hour experience works—especially the **Future → Balikan → 1521 → first monster → artifact discovery → Duwende → Kapre → Cebu → Lapu-Lapu → Mactan → boss → return to Future** loop—you'll have the foundation for the entire BAYANI universe.
